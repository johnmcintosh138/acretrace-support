AcreTrace Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

Support Privacy Policy

AcreTrace does not collect any data about you.

There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code of any kind in the app. Your measurements, notes and photos are stored on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere by the app. Nobody, including the developer, can see them.

This policy explains that in more detail, and covers the two places where information does leave your phone: Apple's map service, and Apple's handling of purchases.

What AcreTrace stores, and where

Surveys, points, project names, notes and any photos you attach are written to the app's own private storage on your iPhone. That storage is not readable by other apps. It is included in your device backup if you back your iPhone up to iCloud or a computer — that backup is yours, and is governed by Apple's terms rather than by this app.

There is no cloud sync and no server behind AcreTrace. Deleting a survey in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes everything at once.

Location

With your permission, AcreTrace reads your location while you're using it, so it can add a measurement point where you're standing and show your position on the map. Location is used on the device to do that, and for nothing else. It is not transmitted, logged, profiled or shared. You can refuse the permission or withdraw it later in Settings; the rest of the app still works, and you place points by hand instead.

Camera and photos

AcreTrace uses the camera for two things: AR tracking, which needs to see the room to measure it, and taking a photo to attach to a survey. Camera frames used for AR tracking are processed live and never recorded. A photo you deliberately take is saved into the app's private storage, at reduced resolution, and stays there until you delete it or the app. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Maps and place search

The map is Apple's MapKit. Displaying it means Apple's servers supply the map imagery for the area you're looking at, and if you search for a place by name, the text you type is sent to Apple to answer the search. That exchange is between you and Apple and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy; Apple states that MapKit does not associate this data with an Apple Account. Your survey points, notes and photos are never part of it — they are not sent to Apple or anyone else.

Purchases

The optional Pro and File Export purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. AcreTrace never sees, receives or stores your payment details, name, email address or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase has been made on the device it is running on, and unlocks the feature if so. Refunds and receipts are handled by Apple.

Files you export or share

When you export a survey as GPX, KML, CSV, a shapefile or a PDF, the file is created on your device and handed to iOS's share sheet. Where it goes from there — Files, Mail, another app, a cloud drive — is your choice, and once it leaves it is subject to that destination's own privacy terms. AcreTrace does not send it anywhere on its own.

Third parties

There are none. AcreTrace contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no third-party libraries. Nothing about your use of the app is shared with anyone.

Children

AcreTrace is not directed at children, and since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data — under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, the CCPA and similar laws — apply to data a company holds about you. AcreTrace holds none, so there is nothing for the developer to retrieve or erase. Your own data is on your own device, where you can view, export or delete it yourself at any time.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently — cloud sync is the change most likely to require it — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change with it.
